Tim Ruffing, Yannick Seurin and I published [DahLIAS](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/692.pdf), the first protocol for full cross-input signature aggregation (CISA) that can reuse Bitcoin's curve, secp256k1.

- Aggregate signatures are 64 bytes.
- Verification is about twice as fast as for half-aggregated Schnorr signatures.
- Signing takes two rounds.

For more background on cross-input signature aggregation in Bitcoin, see Fabian Jahr's [report](https://hrf.org/latest/cisa-research-paper/) for the Human Rights Foundation.
