FROST enables t-of-n wallets, but its distributed key generation (DKG) has been notoriously difficult to implement securely.
My talk at Real World Crypto 2025 presents the [draft ChillDKG BIP](https://github.com/BlockstreamResearch/bip-frost-dkg) from Blockstream Research as a ready-to-implement solution.
