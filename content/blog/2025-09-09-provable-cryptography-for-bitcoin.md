[Provable Cryptography for Bitcoin: An Introduction](https://github.com/cryptography-camp/workbook/releases) is a workbook that teaches formal security definitions and proofs through exercises.
Solutions are included.

The workbook was tested during a multi-day workshop, and I thank the participants for their feedback and contributions.
Nadav Kohen's [prework material](https://github.com/cryptography-camp/prework) is a good preparation.
For more context, see the [Delving Bitcoin post](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/provable-cryptography-for-bitcoin-an-introduction-workbook/1974).

Feedback and contributions as GitHub issues and pull requests are welcome.
